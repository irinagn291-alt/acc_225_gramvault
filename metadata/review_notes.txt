Camera permission is requested when the user opens Source and switches to the Scan pane, or when Scan is shown and authorization has not been determined. The usage description string is: "GramVault reads barcodes to seal products into your local vault." If camera access is denied or restricted, the user can type or paste a barcode or URL, use sample chips, or open Settings from the Scan pane. There is no login, no in-app purchase, no user-generated content shared with other users, no account deletion flow, and no App Tracking Transparency prompt. GramVault is a personal food log for people who want to track daily energy and macros from packaged foods. All data stays on the device in Core Data. There is no sign-in and no credentials or demo account are needed. On a cold launch the app shows a splash screen, then opens the native interface. On first launch a full-screen Briefing appears with four pages: Seal every gram, Source and scan, Set the daily lock, and Lock your targets. Tap Next through the pages, edit Energy kcal, Protein g, Carbs g, and Fat g on the last page, then tap Seal and open, or tap Skip with factory set. The main GRAM VAULT screen opens. Tap Source to search Open Food Facts or scan a barcode, select a product, set grams on page one of the Assign wizard, tap Next, choose a slot and optionally enable Future date for planning, then tap Seal. Tap Wish to view the wish shelf. Tap Targets to edit and Seal targets, Re-run briefing, Reset vault, or Contact to open the contact page. Toggle Seal network on the main screen to block remote lookups; tap About seal for details. On Horizon, tap Eat to mark a planned entry as eaten. Swipe to delete wish items or tap Delete on log rows. The app contacts Open Food Facts at world.openfoodfacts.org for product search (/cgi/search.pl) and barcode lookup (/api/v2/product/<code>.json), and may load product image URLs returned by Open Food Facts. The app also opens https://gramvault.pro/contact-us when the user taps Contact on the Targets screen. Food log entries, cached products, wishes, targets, and settings remain on the device in Core Data and UserDefaults. Behaviour does not differ by region; number formatting follows the device locale. No licence or regulated-industry paperwork applies. GramVault is a consumer food log with an on-screen note that it is not medical advice, not a medical device, and not a gambling product.
